<div class="section-header"><h4 data-i18n="laforte.heading">About the Laforte Catalogue</h4></div>

<h2 data-i18n="laforte.h_person">Conrad Laforte</h2>

<p data-i18n-html="laforte.intro">Conrad Laforte (1921–2014) was a Canadian folklorist at Université Laval in Quebec City who devoted his career to preserving and classifying the French-language folk song tradition. His monumental work, the <em>Catalogue de la chanson folklorique française</em> (6 volumes, 1977–1987, Presses de l'Université Laval), remains the definitive reference for Francophone folk music scholarship.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="laforte.h_system">Classification System</h2>

<p data-i18n="laforte.system_intro">The catalogue organizes songs into six broad categories based on their poetic and musical structure, each divided into sub-categories and individually numbered entries.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="laforte.h_number">How to Read a Laforte Number</h2>

<p data-i18n-html="laforte.number_intro">Each entry in the catalogue is identified by a three-part code such as <strong>II-A-1</strong>:</p>

<ul>
  <li data-i18n-html="laforte.number_roman">The Roman numeral identifies the <strong>category</strong> (I–VI above)</li>
  <li data-i18n-html="laforte.number_letter">The letter identifies the <strong>sub-category</strong> (A, B, C…)</li>
  <li data-i18n-html="laforte.number_digit">The final number is the <strong>sequential entry number</strong> within that sub-category</li>
</ul>

<h2 data-i18n="laforte.h_metre">Metre Notation</h2>

<p data-i18n-html="laforte.metre_intro">Each song in the catalogue is assigned a metre code describing the syllabic structure of its verse. The code takes the form <code>type:subtype(syllabic_pattern)</code>, where the type and subtype are numbers indicating the broad metrical class, and the parenthetical describes how many syllables appear in each line of a strophe.</p>
